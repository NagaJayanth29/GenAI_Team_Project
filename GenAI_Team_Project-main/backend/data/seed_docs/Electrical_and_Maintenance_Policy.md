# University Residential Halls — Electrical Safety and Maintenance Protocol (2026)
Approved by: Superintending Engineer (Electrical & Civil) and Council of Wardens
Document ID: DOC-MAINT-2026-03 | Classification: Official Approved Policy

---

## Section 1: Permitted and Prohibited Electrical Appliances
1.1 Permitted Devices: Students are permitted to operate standard personal electronics: laptops, desktop PCs (power rated under 400W), mobile phone chargers, table study lamps (LED/CFL), and low-wattage grooming trimmers.
1.2 Strictly Prohibited Appliances: The following high-power resistive and heating equipment are strictly prohibited in residential rooms:
- Electric room heaters and heat blowers
- Induction cooktops, immersion water heaters, and electric kettles exceeding 500W
- Air conditioners and unauthorized desert coolers
- Electric sandwich toasters and hot plates
1.3 Confiscation and Fines: Prohibited electrical equipment discovered during routine safety inspections will be confiscated without return until the completion of the degree program. An unauthorized appliance penalty of ₹1,500 will be levied.

---

## Section 2: Maintenance Request Workflow and Turnaround Times
2.1 Request Submission: All repairs (electrical, plumbing, carpentry, air circulation, mason work) must be submitted via the HostelIQ Complaints module.
2.2 SLA Guidelines:
- Emergency Issues (Live sparking, water pipe bursts, elevator stalls): Attended within two (2) hours.
- High Priority (Fan failure, tube light replacement, lock malfunctions): Attended within 24 hours.
- Medium/Normal Priority (Furniture touch-up, window mesh repair): Attended within 72 hours.
2.3 Staff Access: Authorized estate maintenance staff will carry official photo identity badges and will only enter rooms when accompanied by a hostel floor supervisor or the room occupant.
