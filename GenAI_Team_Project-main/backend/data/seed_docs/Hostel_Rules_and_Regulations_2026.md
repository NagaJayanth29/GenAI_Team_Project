# University Student Residential Halls — General Rules and Regulations Handbook (Academic Year 2026)
Approved by: Council of Wardens and Dean of Student Affairs
Document ID: DOC-POL-2026-01 | Classification: Official Approved Policy

---

## Section 1: Gate Timings, Curfew, and Entry Protocols
1.1 Main Gate Curfew: The main hostel gates will be locked strictly at 10:30 PM on all weekdays (Monday through Friday) and at 11:00 PM on weekends (Saturday and Sunday).
1.2 Late Entry Gate Pass: Any student arriving past curfew must present a pre-approved Digital Late Entry Pass issued via the Hostel Administration Portal. The request must be submitted at least four (4) hours prior to gate closure and countersigned by the student's research guide or batch coordinator.
1.3 Unapproved Late Arrivals: Students arriving after curfew without prior clearance will have their entry logged in the Warden Disciplinary Register. More than two (2) unapproved late entries in a semester will result in disciplinary review and parental intimation.
1.4 Night Out / Overnight Leave: Overnight leave outside campus requires submission of a "Night-Out Request Form" on the portal 24 hours prior to travel. Verification SMS will be dispatched to the registered guardian contact.

---

## Section 2: Visitor and Guest Policy
2.1 Visiting Hours: Visitors, including parents, local guardians, and fellow students from other hostel blocks, are permitted only in the designated Ground Floor Reception Lounge between 04:30 PM and 08:00 PM on weekdays, and 10:00 AM to 08:00 PM on Sundays and Institute holidays.
2.2 Room Access: No external visitor, parent, or student of a different residential block is permitted inside student living rooms at any time without written authorization from the Resident Warden.
2.3 Overnight Stay Prohibitions: Overnight stay of guests in student rooms is strictly prohibited. Violations will incur an administrative penalty of ₹2,500 and possible suspension from hostel accommodation for the following academic term.
2.4 Guest Rooms: For visiting parents, a limited number of Institute Guest Rooms may be booked via the Estate Office portal at subsidized university tariff rates, subject to room availability.

---

## Section 3: Room Allocation, Furniture, and Inspection
3.1 Room Inventory: At the time of room allotment, each student signs an inventory checklist for: 1 study desk, 1 ergonomic chair, 1 wooden bed frame with mattress, 1 steel wardrobe with lock, and 1 ceiling fan with regulator.
3.2 Damage and Liability: Any defacement of walls, tampering with electrical fixtures, or damage to university property will be charged directly to the caution deposit of the room occupants.
3.3 Room Transfers: Mutual room exchanges or unilateral room shifts without written sanction from the Chief Warden are treated as unauthorized occupancy and will result in cancellation of allotment. Formal room change applications are reviewed only during the first two weeks of each semester.
3.4 Inspection Rights: The Chief Warden, Resident Wardens, and authorized Hostel Supervisors reserve the right to inspect student rooms at reasonable hours to verify compliance with safety, sanitation, and discipline standards.

---

## Section 4: Noise Discipline and Quiet Hours
4.1 Quiet Hours: Strict silence hours are enforced across all hostel wings and corridors from 11:00 PM to 06:00 AM daily.
4.2 Audio Equipment: High-decibel audio speakers, amplifiers, and subwoofers are prohibited in individual rooms. Students must use headphones for music and multimedia after 10:00 PM.
4.3 Common Rooms: Common television and recreation rooms close promptly at 11:30 PM, except during officially approved academic events or university screenings.
