# University Dining Halls — Mess Guidelines, Food Hygiene, and Rebate Policy (2026)
Approved by: Student Mess Committee, Resident Wardens, and Chief Medical Officer
Document ID: DOC-MESS-2026-02 | Classification: Official Approved Policy

---

## Section 1: Dining Hall Operating Timings and Etiquette
1.1 Service Hours:
- Breakfast: 07:30 AM to 09:30 AM (Weekdays) | 08:00 AM to 10:30 AM (Weekends)
- Lunch: 12:15 PM to 02:15 PM (Weekdays) | 12:30 PM to 02:45 PM (Weekends)
- Evening Snacks: 05:00 PM to 06:15 PM (Daily)
- Dinner: 07:45 PM to 09:45 PM (Daily)
1.2 Dining Entry: Every student must tap their Biometric Smart Card or present their authenticated QR code at the dining hall entrance turnstiles.
1.3 Food Removal: Dining hall food and utensils (plates, bowls, glasses, spoons) may not be taken out of the dining hall or into student residential rooms, except in certified medical illness cases endorsed by the University Health Center.

---

## Section 2: Mess Rebate and Absence Deductions
2.1 Eligibility Criteria: Students leaving campus for academic conferences, field projects, or authorized institute holidays are entitled to a daily dining rebate.
2.2 Minimum Duration: A minimum continuous absence of four (4) full dining days is required to qualify for rebate credit.
2.3 Advance Notice Requirement: Rebate applications must be lodged via the HostelIQ portal at least 72 hours (3 days) before the starting meal of absence. Retroactive rebate claims after departure will be rejected automatically.
2.4 Monthly Cap: The maximum allowable rebate in a single calendar month during regular semester instruction is ten (10) days, unless an official medical leave letter is issued by the University CMO.

---

## Section 3: Dietary Standards, Special Needs, and Feedback
3.1 Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian Segregation: All preparation, storage, and serving counters for vegetarian and non-vegetarian food items are strictly segregated with separate cooking utensils.
3.2 Jain Food Requests: Jain diet options (food cooked without onion, garlic, or root vegetables) are served upon semester-start registration with the Dining Supervisor.
3.3 Hygiene Inspections: The Student Mess Committee and Resident Warden conduct bi-weekly microbiological and kitchen swab tests. Test results are published on the hostel notice board.
3.4 Feedback and Grievances: Complaints regarding meal quality or service delays must be submitted through the HostelIQ Complaints portal under the "Mess" category within 24 hours of the meal service.
